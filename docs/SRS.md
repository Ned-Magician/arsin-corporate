# 1. Project Overview

Arsin Industrial Group provides MDF lamination services for furniture workshops and manufacturers.

Customers send their MDF panels (and sometimes decorative paper), and Arsin laminates the material using industrial pressing machines.

The purpose of the website is to present the company professionally, build trust, and encourage potential customers to contact the company for quotations and business cooperation.
